Connection tips & performance fixes

If you ever face slow speeds or connection issues, these quick optimizations usually help:

  • Switch servers: try another location if a server feels congested
  • Choose a nearby server: closer servers typically deliver better speed and lower latency
  • Toggle airplane mode: refreshing your network often improves VPN connectivity
  • Check background apps: heavy downloads or streaming apps can affect VPN speed
  • Ensure a stable connection: VPN performance depends on your underlying Wi-Fi or mobile network
