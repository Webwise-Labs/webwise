Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Webwise VPN account and associated data. Webwise VPN is developed by Webwise Labs LLC.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion, email us from your registered email address.

  1. Send an email to hello@webwiselabs.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered email address in the message
Processing time: We process account deletion requests within 30 days of verification.

We may request additional verification to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

What data will be deleted

  • Account email address
  • Account identifier
  • Associated account preferences (if any)

What data Webwise does not collect

  • Browsing history
  • VPN traffic
  • DNS queries
  • Websites or URLs you visit

Webwise follows a strict no-logs approach.

Data retention

  • Anonymous, non-identifiable analytics may be retained for product improvement.
  • Billing and refund handling is managed by Google Play / Apple App Store and records may be retained as required by applicable law.

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion or privacy, contact us at hello@webwiselabs.com.
