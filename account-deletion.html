Account Deletion

For Webwise VPN by Webwise Labs LLC

How to request account deletion

  1. Email us at hello@webwiselabs.com
  2. Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Send the request from your registered email address
Processing time: We process deletion requests within 30 days of verification.

We may request additional verification to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

What data will be deleted

  • Account email address
  • Account identifier
  • Associated account preferences

What data we do NOT collect

  • Browsing history
  • VPN traffic
  • DNS queries
  • Websites or URLs you visit

Webwise follows a strict no-logs policy.

Data retention

  • Anonymous, non-identifiable analytics may be retained
  • Billing records are handled by Google Play / Apple App Store as required by law

Questions? Contact hello@webwiselabs.com
